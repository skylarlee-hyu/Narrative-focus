# Replication checklist

- [ ] Lock final sample: 48 KOSPI-listed firms, news record totals and unique article counts.
- [ ] Record newspaper coverage, source licensing, deduplication and trading-calendar construction.
- [ ] Freeze dictionaries of executive names and titles, including historical changes.
- [ ] Document how headlines with no leader mention are classified and how ambiguous mentions are handled.
- [ ] Validate rule robustness using alternative name-only, title-only and strict classification.
- [ ] Record exact Korean sentiment-model checkpoint and transformation into valence and tone.
- [ ] Verify benchmark: KOSPI versus matched total-return index and corporate-action adjustment.
- [ ] Align publication dates, after-close reports and next-trading-day return windows.
- [ ] Reconstruct BHARs at 1, 3, 5, 10, 15 and 30 trading days.
- [ ] Finalize controls, fixed effects, clustered errors and overlapping-event handling.
- [ ] Test tech versus non-tech differences directly; do not infer from differential significance.
- [ ] Verify investment strategy timing and transaction costs.
- [ ] Reproduce paper tables and figures from finalized scripts.
- [ ] Review licensing before sharing any underlying news or market-price data.
